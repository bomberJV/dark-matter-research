04 / What the change reveals

One observation.
A very different expectation.

In this calculation, the endothermic higgsino benchmark depends strongly on the fast tail of the assumed halo. Raising the cutoff opens much more of that tail. The exothermic benchmark releases internal energy and changes little here.

The observation has not moved. The experiment has not recorded more events. This comparison exposes a model’s sensitivity to an assumption; it does not establish either explanation.

The reported observation Energy, exposure and primary sources

The reported candidate energy is 248 ± 23 (statistical) ± 23 (systematic) keV. These uncertainties are not combined here. The event date is 2023-06-16; the reported exposure is 2.84 tonne-years.

The paper reports 2.6σ global tension and a maximum local tension of 3.4σ for the tested background comparisons. These are not discovery significance or probabilities that either selected explanation is correct. No measured event position, waveform or detector reconstruction is exported.

  • LZ primary paper, v1 — reported observation and uncertainty.
  • Rodd, Safdi, Slatyer & Xu, v1 — selected higgsino benchmark.
  • Baer & Barger, v1 — selected 300 GeV exothermic benchmark.

The official data release was not retrieved or verified. Article figures are link-only; this experience copies none of them.

What changed, and what stayed fixed Exactly one input changes

Only the Galactic escape speed changes: 544 → 600 km/s. This is an assumption comparison, not a fitted value, an uncertainty interval or a measurement of the halo. The observation, exposure, cross-sections, masses, splittings and detector model stay fixed.

Approximations and unresolved differences Numerical reproduction ≠ paper reproduction

The detector efficiency is piecewise linear, with support from 4 to 285 keV. The 5.4 and 269.9 keV points are 50% efficiency points, not hard selection boundaries. The Gaussian resolution is σ(E) = √[23²E/248 + (0.093E)²] keV. Background is neglected. Sideband acceptance is unpublished and assumed to be one.
