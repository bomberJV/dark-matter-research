What changes—and what does not

The endothermic benchmark depends strongly on the fastest halo particles. At its unchanged published cross-section, raising the escape speed can increase the absolute predicted count while reducing the number of sideband events per ROI event. The exothermic benchmark is much less sensitive in this comparison. These are conditional model calculations, not evidence that either model explains the candidate.

Both calculations use natural xenon, a Helm form factor, approximate efficiency, the same energy resolution and neglected background. The true-energy efficiency has support from 4 to 285 keV; 5.4 and 269.9 keV are its 50% efficiency points, not hard integration boundaries. The official event-level data release was not checked for this export.
