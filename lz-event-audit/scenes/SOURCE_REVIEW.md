The other catalog entries preserve the original audit's source IDs, claims,
halo descriptions and notes. They have not all received a new primary-paper
review in this slice. The inferred sign for `gce-simple`, converted sign for
`fan-he-bestfit`, non-reproductions for `su-yang-yang` and `dent-newstead-exo`,
and substantial Delepine-Khalil discrepancies must remain inspectable.

The manifest's scientific validity is bounded by the calculation assumptions.
Numerical convergence cannot remove efficiency uncertainty, unknown sideband
acceptance or a disagreement with a source. The candidate does not establish
dark matter and the comparison does not rank a best theory.
