What this investigation demonstrates

Same evidence.
Different expectations.

In this calculation, the endothermic higgsino benchmark depends strongly on the fast tail of the assumed halo. Raising the cutoff makes more of that tail available. The exothermic benchmark releases internal energy and changes little here.

The observation has not moved. The experiment has not recorded more events. The result exposes sensitivity to an assumption; it does not establish either explanation.
